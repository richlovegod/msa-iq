    • 唯一在跑的 Ph3：Lundbeck amlenetug（MASCOT，主要完成估 2028/02）• The only Ph3 running: Lundbeck's amlenetug (MASCOT, primary completion est. 2028/02)

    • 正面 Ph2 訊號：Alterity ATH434、小野 ONO-2808（均含次族群／公司口徑限制）• Positive Ph2 signals: Alterity's ATH434 and Ono's ONO-2808 (both with subgroup/company-framing caveats)

    • 已知失敗與退場：Biohaven verdiperstat Ph3 未達終點（2021）、Biogen 終止 ION464（2025/02）• Known failures and exits: Biohaven's verdiperstat missed in Ph3 (2021); Biogen discontinued ION464 (2025/02)

    • 細胞治療：Mayo 鞘內自體 MSC 的隨機對照 Ph2 進行中，是此路線的關鍵驗證• Cell therapy: Mayo's randomised, controlled Ph2 of intrathecal autologous MSC is the pivotal test for this route

    → 量表爭議是共同主題：UMSARS 總分作為主要終點的靈敏度不足，近期設計普遍轉向改良版 UMSARS 與更早期病人。→ A shared theme is the endpoint debate: total UMSARS lacks sensitivity as a primary endpoint, and recent designs have shifted to the modified UMSARS and earlier patients.
